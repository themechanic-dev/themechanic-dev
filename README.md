### by the mechanic

A one-person home lab: desktop and phone apps that put servers, updates and AI assistants in one window — for Linux, Windows and Android.

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Proxmox Controller](https://github.com/themechanic-dev/claude-proxmox-controller) | Your Proxmox VE hosts in one desktop window, and Claude working on them only as far as you let it. · [page](https://themechanic.store/controller) |
| [Voice Console](https://github.com/themechanic-dev/claude-code-voice-console) | Talk to Claude Code by voice and hear it answer, on your computer and from your phone. · [page](https://themechanic.store/voice-console) |
| [Routine Panel](https://github.com/themechanic-dev/routine-panel) | Every routine and automation of your computer in one window, with a phone app. · [page](https://themechanic.store/routine-panel/) |
| [Atomic Atlas](https://github.com/themechanic-dev/atomic-atlas) | An educational simulator of what nuclear explosions and reactor accidents do, built on public science only. · [page](https://themechanic.store/atomic-atlas) |
| [Update Ubuntu via terminal](https://github.com/themechanic-dev/update-ubuntu-via-terminal) | Everything on Ubuntu up to date from one place: apt, Snap, Flatpak, AppImages, and other machines over SSH. |
| [Server Controller](https://github.com/themechanic-dev/server-controller) | A GNOME app for servers with an IPMI BMC: power, sensors, history charts, fan modes. |
| [Steganon VPN](https://github.com/themechanic-dev/steganon-vpn) | Kill switch and automatic failover for OpenVPN, on Linux and Windows. |
